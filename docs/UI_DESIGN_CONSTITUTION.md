# Nexus UI Design Constitution

## Direction

Nexus is a precision enterprise operations workspace. It should feel calm,
reliable and exact, like professional laboratory software, not a game HUD,
marketing site or AI demonstration.

## Core Rules

1. One primary action per work surface. Secondary actions use outline, ghost or menus.
2. Use spacing, dividers and typography before adding a card. Never nest cards.
3. Gradients and glow are not allowed on authenticated business screens.
4. Pills are reserved for status, tags and filters. Controls use 4-8px radii.
5. AI is identified by language and evidence, not purple color or magic icons.
6. Business users see status, evidence, confidence and control. Full traces stay on demand.
7. Hover changes color or border only. Content does not float, bounce, rotate or scale.
8. Motion is limited to state transitions, dialogs, drawers, loading and progress.
9. Page titles are 20-24px; panel titles are 14-16px. Letter spacing remains zero.
10. Tables and action lists are the default for repeated operational work.

## Visual Tokens

- Radius: 4px compact controls, 6px inputs, 8px panels and overlays.
- Elevation: no shadow for page sections; subtle shadow for menus; elevated shadow for dialogs.
- Color: neutral surfaces, cobalt primary action, semantic green/amber/red only for status.
- Typography: IBM Plex Sans / Noto Sans SC with IBM Plex Mono for identifiers and measurements.
- Motion: 120-180ms for controls, 180-240ms for overlays, no decorative infinite motion.

## Review Checklist

- Can a user identify the next action in five seconds?
- Is there more than one primary button in the same visual group?
- Can any panel be replaced by a divider or plain section?
- Does AI content explain its evidence instead of displaying internal reasoning?
- Are empty states short and actionable rather than promotional?
- Does the page remain clear at 1280px, 1440px, 1920px and mobile width?
